How to Play

Fill each square with a Survivor castaway who matches both the row and column clues.

  • Each answer can only be used once in the grid.
  • You have 9 total guesses, so make each pick count.
  • When the puzzle ends, use the copy button to share your result anywhere you like.
  • Each correct answer shows a rarity score based on the percentage of players who chose that castaway for that cell. A higher number means a more popular answer.
  • The daily puzzle resets at 6:00 AM Eastern Time (ET), regardless of your local time zone.
  • Daily puzzle difficulty varies by day of the week: Wednesday is generally the easiest, then it scales up through the week to the hardest puzzle on Tuesday.
  • Data for seasons that are currently airing is not updated until those seasons are over, to help avoid spoilers.

Attributes

  • Season: players who appeared in a specific season, era, location, or season-themed clue.
  • Placement: players who finished in a specific placement (for example, Sole Survivor, Runner-Up, or Third Place).
  • Milestones: players who reached (or did not reach) major game stages like Merge, Jury, or Final Tribal Council.
  • Returning Player: players who competed on more than one season.
  • Advantages: players who received, held, used, or successfully/unsuccessfully played an advantage.
  • Immunity Wins: players with at least the listed number of individual or career immunity wins.
  • Confessionals: players with at least the listed number of career confessionals.
  • Votes Against: players who received at least the listed number of votes against them across their career.
  • Sit-Outs: players with at least the listed number of times they sat out of challenges across their career.
  • Tribe Color: players who were on a tribe with the listed buff color (grouped by color family).
  • Firemaking: players who won or lost a firemaking tiebreaker challenge.
